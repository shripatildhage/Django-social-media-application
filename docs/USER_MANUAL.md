# User Manual

**Sign up / log in** - use *Sign up* (username, email, password) then you are logged in automatically.

**Posting** - type in the box on Home, optionally attach an image (max 5 MB, JPG/PNG/GIF/WebP), press *Post*. Delete your own posts with *Delete*.

**Feed** - *Following* shows you, people you follow and friends; *Discover* shows all public posts.

**Likes & comments** - click *Likes* to toggle; open *Comments* to read or reply.

**Profile** - `Edit profile` to set name, bio, avatar, cover photo, location, website. Tick *Is private* so only followers/friends see your posts.

**Follow vs friends** - *Follow* is one-way. *Add friend* sends a request; the other person accepts or declines under *Friends*.

**Notifications** - the bell badge updates instantly when someone likes, comments, follows or sends a request. Open *Notifications* to read, or *Mark all read*.

**Search** - use the top bar to find people and posts.

**Report** - on a post page, enter a reason and press *Report*; moderators review it.
